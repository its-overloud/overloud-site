About OVER-LOUD

OVER-LOUD is a voice synthesis development circle dedicated to creating and releasing original projects.

We operate and develop a range of our own voicebanks, characters, and tools, with a special emphasis on detail and artistry behind the scenes.

We treat each project as a complete creative work. Every phoneme is carefully considered, our characters are designed with intention and every release is polished until it meets our standards. We believe synthesized voices deserve the same craft and care as any other form of musical and artistic expression.

Our goal is to build voices that sound natural, carry personality, and give creators new ways to bring their ideas to life.
