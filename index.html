About OVER-LOUD

OVER-LOUD is a voice synthesis circle devoted to original projects, our own voicebanks, characters, and tools, crafted with genuine passion.

Every detail was lingered over, every character shaped with intention, and nothing leaves our hands until it earns its place. A synthesized voice, we believe, deserves the same terrible patience and care as any other art.

Our aim: Natural voices that carry a presence and give creators new ways to bring their ideas into the world.
